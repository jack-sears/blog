Been playing fpl for a while now and wanted to mess around with Claude to see what kind of visuals I could create to help make decisions about my team. Below is my current team and I have 5 free transfers.

![team](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw11/gw11team.png)

I'll use the visuals to discuss general thoughts and then narrow in on how they will affect my decision making for my own team.

## Teams

We can start by looking at expected goals and expected goals against per team:

![teamXG](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw11/gw10_team_attack.png)

Brighton and Man City appear to be putting up the best underlying numbers. Hull and Coventry are consistently the worst attacks in the Prem. Spurs, Palace, Leeds, and Fulham are sub par attacks only because they are underperforming their xG. Forest and Sunderland putting up good numbers but are TERRIBLY underperforming their xG. I would be cautious against them since either may be due for a bunch of goals if they gain some momentum in front of goal. Everton up until Brentford are all solid attacks and I would say targeting fixtures or talismen for these teams are fine ideas. Chelsea are interesting as they are overperforming their xG, but they also seem to be one of the most reliable teams as they feel predictable in terms of where the goals will come from (Rogers, Palmer, JP).

And for xGA:

![teamXGA](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw11/gw10_team_defence.png)

Arsenal are clearly the best defense. Everyone else in the middle block from Brighton up until Bournemouth look good, while Chelsea until Ipswich less so. Pretty much, have 1-2 Arsenal DEFs, target the middle blocks fixtures, the upper blocks fixtures for differential, and captain any player who plays palace lol.

Next is the goals conceded and scored charts. These give a better insight into extremes, that is, which teams are often scoring lots or little and same thing for conceding. Pretty straight forward.

![teamBar](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw11/gw10_team_cs_goals.png)

I used the above graphic and the xG and xGA graphics to choose defense and attack ratings (1-5, higher the better) for each team. 

## Fixtures

Using the ratings mentioned above, I arrive at my own Fixture Difficulty Rating (FDR) graphic:

![fdr](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw11/gw11_fdr_gw11_15.png)

Looking at my team and the FDR, let's go through each position. I am happy with my GK's as Brighton and Spurs are towards the top of the defensive fixtures.

For defenders, I think Gabriel is interesting, since I already have 2 arsenal defenders. Upgrading one of them would feel a little out of place since Arsenal have tough upcoming fixtures. That being said Gabriel is nailed and will get points, but I think aslong as one of Califiori or Konsa start (or both) I am fine with it for the short term. I may Free Hit in GW12 (Arsenal vs City) and then wildcard soon after, so bringing in Gabriel really is not a problem either. Virg has good fixtures. Gvardiol and Ajer are fine, but I think these two can be upgraded more than anyone else in the team.

Ndiaye and Tavernier both could go, Ndiaye most likely as Tavernier plays an underperforming Forest and although his fixtures are not great, he is putting up good numbers and bournemouth seem to be scoring as well. Gomez doesn't feel great as Gross is just a better option. Mbeumo feels good with a potential manager bounce and then reassess in GW12 or 13 after free hit. Keeping rogers until Palace seems smart as well.

That leaves attackers. Haaland, Isak, and Jackson are going nowwhere for the foreseeable future. Between bagging goals and fixtures I like all three.
