## Teams
A small addition to the team attack and defense charts. I have added change in xG over the past 3 games via arrows, while color coding their fixture difficulty. This should help us get a better of idea of where teams are headed and why.

![Team xG vs goals, coloured by difficulty of last three opponents, with trend arrows](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw12/gw11_team_attack_trend.png)

<!-- Talking points -->

Nothing new here but it is cool to see teams like Liverpool and Bournemouth taking advantage of easier fixtures while City have struggled with tougher, setting all three on a path to what almost looks like the same ending point. I guess we can also all laugh at Sunderland as they continue to improve xG wise while scoring less and less goals...

![Team xGA vs goals conceded, coloured by difficulty of last three opponents, with trend arrows](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw12/gw11_team_defence_trend.png)

<!-- Talking points -->

No surprises here, Arsenal with tougher fixtures and potentially no Raya raises a few eyebrows, Leeds and Tottenham continue to impress, and Liverpool, Brentford, Brighton, and Bournemouth are doing well. Palace, United, and Ipswich are still the big 3 but not in a good way.

## Fixtures: GW12–16, Including Europe and Cups

I have updated the FDR to include European and Cup fixtures which should be considered when making transfers.

![Fixture difficulty GW12–16 with European and Carabao Cup games](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw12/gw12_fdr_gw12_16_midweek.png)

I think this is a very important time, as the next 5 fixtures happen over the following 2 weeks. Arsenal for example have Tottenham (A) in 14, Madrid in the UCL mid week, and then Bournemouth(H). Two decent premier league fixtures but for a player like Califiori or other rotation risks, it could get ugly. Leeds for example, although play City (A) midweek in 13, with no Europe the following week you can be confident in player minutes vs Ipswich (H) in 14. I just think it's worth considering before making transfers.

## Forwards

![Forwards: goals vs xG per 90, coloured by share of team xG](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw12/gw11_fwd_goals_share_p90.png)

<!-- Talking points -->

![Forwards: assists vs xA per 90, coloured by share of team xA](https://jack-sears.github.io/blog/assets/images/fplgw12/gw11_fwd_assists_share_p90.png)

<!-- Talking points -->

We can see clear forward picks emerging. Haaland and Isak are living up to their names with Barry, Evanilson, Jackson, Brobbey, and DCL offering cheaper options. Nico Jackson is putting up the worst underlying xG but his xA and fixtures are good. He will probably be the forward to swap soon and it'll most likely be back to JP. DCL is also putting up some solid numbers and being the only pen taker on the list outside of Haaland and Isak, really should not be ignored.  I am not a big fan of xA for forwards, as it just seems much less reliable, so I stick mostly to xG to help make decisions.
